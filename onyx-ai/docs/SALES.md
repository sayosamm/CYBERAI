# Onyx AI — Sales Kit

Everything you need to go from "cold list" to "signed client." Scripts are
copy-paste ready — tweak the `[brackets]`. Lead with the customer's pain (missed
calls = lost jobs), show the demo, then ask for the sale.

> **Golden rule:** You're not selling "AI." You're selling **booked jobs they're
> currently losing.** Talk money and time, not tech.

---

## 1. The pitch (memorize these)

**One-liner:**
> "I set up an AI that answers your phone and texts back missed callers, so you
> stop losing jobs when you can't get to the phone."

**30-second version:**
> "You know how when you're on a job, calls go to voicemail — and most people
> don't leave one, they just call the next guy? I set up an AI receptionist that
> answers every call and instantly texts back anyone you miss, finds out what
> they need, and books them. It runs 24/7. I build the whole thing for you, and
> it's $299 a month."

**Value in one sentence:** *Never lose another job to a missed call.*

---

## 2. Who to call (build your list — 15 min)

Open Google Maps. Search your city + each trade. Make a list of 20–30:
- HVAC / heating & air
- Plumbers
- Electricians
- Cleaning companies / maid services
- Auto detailers
- (Also good: garage doors, pest control, landscaping, roofing, locksmiths)

**Best targets:** small owner-operated shops (1–10 trucks). Look for ones with a
cell number listed, a basic/old website, or lots of reviews mentioning
"couldn't get ahold of them" / "never called back." Those are gold.

Put them in a simple sheet: **Business | Trade | Phone | Owner name | Status | Notes**

---

## 3. Cold call script

> **You:** "Hey, is this the owner?"
> **Them:** "Yeah, this is [Name]."
> **You:** "Hey [Name], my name's [You] — I'll be quick. I help [plumbers] stop
> losing jobs from missed calls. Real quick — when you're out on a job and the
> phone rings, what usually happens to that call?"
> **Them:** "Goes to voicemail / my wife gets it / I miss it."
> **You:** "Right, and most of those people just call the next guy, yeah? That's
> the problem I fix. I set up an AI that answers your calls and texts back
> anyone you miss within a minute — finds out what they need and books them.
> Runs 24/7, I set the whole thing up. Can I text you a 60-second demo so you
> can see it?"
> **Them:** "Sure."
> **You:** "Perfect, what's the best number? … Sent. Try it like you're a
> customer — ask it anything. I'll follow up tomorrow. Sound good?"

**Keys:** Ask the pain question and *let them answer* — they sell themselves.
Keep it under 90 seconds. Goal of the call = get the demo link in their hands.

---

## 4. Walk-in script (even stronger for local)

Walk in during a slow time. Ask for the owner.
> "Hey [Name], I'll be quick — I build AI receptionists for [HVAC] shops around
> here. Can I show you something on my phone real fast? … This answers your
> calls and texts back the ones you miss so you stop losing those jobs. Here —
> ask it a question like you're a customer."

Hand them your phone with the demo open. **Let them play with it.** When they
smile: *"I set this up for your shop, $299 a month. Want me to get yours going
this week?"*

## 8. The demo flow (what to show)

1. **Hook with pain:** "How many calls you think you miss a week?"
2. **Hand them the chat** (your link or your phone): "Ask it anything, like a
   customer would." Let it answer instantly.
3. **The money shot:** "When someone calls and you don't pick up, it texts them
   right back and books them — that's the job you were losing."
4. **Show the dashboard:** "Every lead lands here, and in your inbox."
5. **Close:** "$299 a month, I build and run the whole thing. I can have yours
   live this week — want to start?"

Keep it to ~5 minutes. Don't over-explain the tech — show outcomes.

## 9. Objection handling

**"I already have someone answering the phone."**
> "Perfect — this backs them up. It catches the calls they miss: lunch, after
> hours, when it's slammed. Those are the ones costing you jobs right now."

**"Is it complicated / I'm not techy."**
> "You do nothing. I set the whole thing up and you just get the leads in your
> phone. If you can read a text, you can use it."

**"It's too expensive / $299 is a lot."**
> "Totally fair. One booked job a month more than covers it — everything after
> that is profit. How much is one [HVAC repair / install] worth to you?"
> (Let them say a number — it's always way more than $299.)

**"Does it sound like a robot? Will it annoy my customers?"**
> "It tells people it's an assistant, keeps it short and friendly, and hands off
> to a real person anytime they ask. Want to hear it? Ask it to talk to a human."

**"I don't get many missed calls."**
> "That's easy to find out — most owners are shocked. Even one or two a week at
> [job value] adds up fast. Worst case, it also answers your website and after-
> hours stuff."

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Of course. Tell you what — I'll set yours up so you can watch it catch real
> calls for yourself. If it's not booking you jobs, we stop, no hard feelings.
> Fair?"

**"Can you do it cheaper?"**
> "The monthly's $299 flat. Since you'd be one of my first local clients, I can
> work with you on the setup fee to get you started this week."
> *(This is where you negotiate the setup — see pricing below.)*

**"Send me some info."** (usually a soft no)
> "Happy to — but honestly the demo says it better than any PDF. Let me text you
> the link right now and give you a quick call tomorrow after you've tried it."

## 10. Pricing & the close

- **Public price:** $4,000 one-time setup + **$299/month**.
- **Negotiate the setup in conversation** — the monthly stays $299.
- **Founding-client move (first 1–3 clients):** discount the setup hard
  ($500–$1,500, or even defer it) in exchange for a **testimonial + permission
  to use them as a case study.** Proof is worth more than the setup cash right
  now. Say: *"Because you'd be one of my first clients, I'll do the setup at
  [number] if we start this week and you give me a quick testimonial once you
  see it working."*
- **Always create urgency:** "this week," "one of my first local spots."
- **Ask for the sale directly:** "Want me to get yours going?" Then **stop
  talking** and let them answer.

**Collect the deposit before you build** — Stripe payment link, Zelle, or Cash
App. Money first, work second.

---

## 11. Follow-up cadence (most sales are in the follow-up)

- **Day 0:** Call/walk-in → send personalized demo link.
- **Day 1:** "Did you get a chance to try the demo? What'd you think?"
- **Day 3:** Value nudge: "Thinking about [Business] — even catching 2 missed
  calls a week is a few extra jobs a month. Want me to set it up?"
- **Day 7:** Soft close: "Still happy to get you going this week — want me to?"
- **Day 14:** Break-up text: "I'll stop bugging you 🙂 — if you ever want to stop
  losing jobs to missed calls, I'm one text away."

Track every touch in your sheet. A "no" today is often a "yes" in 3 weeks.

---

## 12. What you promise (and deliver)

- "I build and set the whole thing up for you."
- "You'll get leads in your phone/inbox the moment they come in."
- "A real person is always one tap away for your customers."
- "Nothing goes live until you approve it."
- **Never** promise a specific revenue number or guarantee results. Sell
  *captured leads + saved time*, which the demo proves — not a dollar figure.

---

## Daily routine to land your first client

1. Add 10 new businesses to your list (10 min).
2. Reach out to 10 (calls/walk-ins/DMs) with the pitch (1 hr).
3. Send a personalized demo link to everyone interested.
4. Follow up with yesterday's contacts.
5. Repeat. **Volume + follow-up = your first yes.** 💪
